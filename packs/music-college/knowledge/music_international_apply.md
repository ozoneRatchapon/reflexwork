## How do international applicants apply?

คำค้นภาษาไทย: นักศึกษาต่างชาติ ต่างประเทศ

There are two ways to apply to the College:
- online, through the COMMAS admission portal: https://commas.music.mahidol.ac.th/portal/Admission
- at an international audition: the College holds auditions every year in several Southeast Asian countries; the places and dates are announced on the College's website and social media.
Most programmes and majors are taught in English. Applicants abroad may send a video audition for some majors; each major's admission page gives its own video rules.
International Student Service Center (admissions, travel, housing, settling in): msinteradmission@mahidol.ac.th
