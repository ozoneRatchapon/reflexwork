## Bachelor tuition fees by major (per year and 4-year total)

คำค้น: undergraduate tuition fees cost how much per year ภาษาอังกฤษ

Tuition fees as each major's page states them: Year 1 / Year 2 / Year 3 / Year 4 = 4-year total.
- Classical Music Performance (Guitar, Piano, Harp, Percussion): 183,500 / 164,600 / 182,800 / 161,200 = 692,100
- Classical Music Performance (String except Harp, Woodwind, Brass): 193,500 / 174,600 / 192,800 / 171,200 = 732,100
- Classical Music Performance (Voice): 196,200 / 180,000 / 182,000 / 173,900 = 732,100
- Jazz: 188,900 / 186,200 / 168,000 / 141,000 = 684,100
- Popular Music: 183,500 / 180,800 / 178,800 / 141,000 = 684,100
- Music Composition: 183,500 / 175,400 / 178,200 / 137,000 = 674,100
- Musical Theater: 204,300 / 190,800 / 177,300 / 155,700 = 728,100
- Thai and Eastern Music: 183,500 / 167,300 / 177,400 / 159,900 = 688,100
- Music Education and Pedagogy (Western Music): 196,300 / 174,800 / 174,800 / 105,200 = 651,100; String, Woodwind, Brass, Voice: 206,300 / 184,800 / 174,800 / 105,200 = 671,100; Thai Music: 196,300 / 177,500 / 166,700 / 110,600 = 651,100
- Music Business (Western Music): 199,700 / 178,200 / 145,700 / 102,500 = 626,100; String, Woodwind, Brass, Voice, Voice Musical Theater: 209,700 / 188,200 / 145,700 / 102,500 = 646,100; Thai Music: 107,900 / 180,900 / 137,600 / 107,900 = 626,100
- Music Technology (Western Music): 197,000 / 167,400 / 153,800 / 107,900 = 626,100; String, Woodwind, Brass, Voice, Voice Musical Theater: 206,200 / 177,400 / 153,800 / 107,900 = 646,100; Thai Music: 107,900 / 170,100 / 148,400 / 110,600 = 626,100
Three rows do not add up to the stated total: Music Business (Thai Music) adds up to 534,300, Music Technology (Thai Music) to 537,000 and Music Technology (String, Woodwind, Brass, Voice) to 645,300. The pages state no currency and no academic year. Please confirm with the admissions office.
