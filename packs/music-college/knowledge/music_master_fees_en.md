## Master's degree tuition fees (Thai and international students)

คำค้น: master's degree masters tuition international student ภาษาอังกฤษ

Master of Music, every major (Jazz, Composition, Conducting, Musical Theatre, Music Performance and Pedagogy, Collaborative Piano, Thai Music):
- Thai students: Year 1 175,000 · Year 2 175,000 · all program 350,000
- International students: Year 1 195,000 · Year 2 195,000 · all program 390,000
Master of Arts (Music Business, Music Education, Music Therapy, Musicology), as Year 1 · Year 2 · Work-Site Study Fee · all program:
- Thai students: 135,000 · 135,000 · 70,000 · 340,000
- International students: 155,000 · 155,000 · 70,000 · 380,000 (except Music Business, regular program: 135,000 · 135,000 · 70,000 · 340,000)
- Music Business, Special Program: Thai students 165,000 · 165,000 · 70,000 · 400,000; international students 185,000 · 185,000 · 70,000 · 440,000
The pages say the fees may change by announcement of the Faculty of Graduate Studies, Mahidol University. They state no currency and no academic year.
