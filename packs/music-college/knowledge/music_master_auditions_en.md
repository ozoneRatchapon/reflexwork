## Master's degree entrance exam by major: audition, written exam, interview

คำค้น: master's entrance exam requirements repertoire what to prepare ภาษาอังกฤษ

What each master's major examines (the full piece lists and video rules are on each major's page, linked from https://www.music.mahidol.ac.th/academic/admission-master/):
- Music Performance and Pedagogy (classical): an audition by instrument group (guitar, voice, music theatre, woodwind, strings, harp, brass, percussion, piano, Thai instrument), e.g. guitar at least 3 pieces, not less than 15 minutes and not over 20, from memory; piano also has a department interview in English. Brass and percussion audition in person.
- Musical Theatre: two vocal exercises, six songs, two contrasting monologues in English (less than 4 minutes total) and a ninety-second to two-minute dance piece.
- Thai Music: an audition on the applicant's instrument or Thai voice (set songs, e.g. at least 32 measures each), judged on completeness, sound quality and technique.
- Collaborative Piano: an instrumental sonata, art songs and an aria, an etude from memory, sight-reading, then a department interview in English. Applicants bring their own partners or ask the College to provide one.
- Jazz: two standards the committee picks from a list of 15, scales and arpeggios in all keys, sight-reading, all from memory.
- Conducting: a pre-screening video of at least 30 minutes (rehearsal and performance), then a written exam, an aural skills exam with interview, and conducting a live ensemble.
- Composition: three original compositions sent with the application, then a 10-15 minute presentation in English, an audition (scales, arpeggios, chord progressions) and an interview.
- Musicology: a written exam, 120 minutes, essay questions. No audition.
- Music Therapy: at least 1 hours of observing therapy sessions before the exams (with a written observation), a 2-hour written exam in Thai and English, an audition of 20-30 minutes per person and an interview.
- Music Education: a research proposal, a 15-minute video of the applicant teaching at least 2 students and its lesson plan, then a written exam.
- Music Business: a paper of not over 10 pages (a business plan or a research proposal), then an interview.
Every major also asks for an English score (see the graduate English score entry) and a music theory and history placement exam. Applicants abroad: several majors accept a video or an online exam; each page says which. The major pages state no academic year.
