## Admission rounds for 2027 entry: deadlines, audition, interview and results dates

คำค้น: when audition date interview exam results announced application deadline register report pay tuition schedule rounds ภาษาอังกฤษ

Academic year 2027 (2570) has four rounds. Every round is open to all programmes and majors (Pre-College, Bachelor, Graduate) and starts in the 1st semester (August 2027). Applications have been open since August 25, 2026. The audition and major tests are held on site. Round 1 closed on 19 Sep. 2026.
Round 2
- Application deadline: Sat. 7 Nov. 2026
- List of applicants with exam date, time and room: Sat. 14 Nov. 2026
- Audition and major test: Sat. 28 Nov. 2026
- List of applicants called to interview: Wed. 9 Dec. 2026
- Interview: Sat. 12 Dec. 2026
- Results: Sat. 19 Dec. 2026
- Report, register and pay tuition fees: 19–25 Dec. 2026
Round 3
- Application deadline: Sat. 9 Jan. 2027
- List of applicants with exam date, time and room: Sat. 16 Jan. 2027
- Audition and major test: Sat. 30 Jan. 2027
- List of applicants called to interview: Wed. 10 Feb. 2027
- Interview: Sat. 13 Feb. 2027
- Results: Sat. 20 Feb. 2027
- Report, register and pay tuition fees: 20–26 Feb. 2027
Round 4
- Application deadline: Sat. 27 Feb. 2027
- List of applicants with exam date, time and room: Sat. 6 Mar. 2027
- Audition and major test: Sat. 20 Mar. 2027
- List of applicants called to interview: Wed. 31 Mar. 2027
- Interview: Sat. 3 Apr. 2027
- Results: Sat. 10 Apr. 2027
- Report, register and pay tuition fees: 10–22 Apr. 2027
Results are posted on COMMAS: https://commas.music.mahidol.ac.th
