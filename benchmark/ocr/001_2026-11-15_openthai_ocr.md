# 001 — OpenThai2.0-OCR-Text on Mahidol College of Music site images (2026-11-15)

Verdict: usable as a draft aid with mandatory human review, not as an unattended source. Clean Thai text reads well: rendered controls CER 1.1%, the real clean announcement graphic 2.4%, short Thai blocks cropped from posters 1.6-3.1%. But a busy poster loses whole lines in full-image mode, large coloured date numerals are dropped, and display fonts flip Thai letters to Latin look-alikes (พ→w, น→U, ร→S). Dates and numbers, the facts a knowledge entry most needs, are where it fails. Real-image overall CER is 8.0% (Thai-dominant subset 7.1%, English posters 8.6%). Measured on 4 controls plus 10 hand-transcribed real inputs. The sample is small.

## Method

- Model: `iapp/OpenThai2.0-OCR-Text` at commit `40e8e9f` (Apache-2.0). Files used: `det.onnx`, `rec.onnx`, `rec_dict.txt`, `det.yml`, `config.json`. SHA-256 sums matched the repo's `SHA256SUMS`. Downloads went to `/tmp/ocr_bench/dl/` and were treated as untrusted.
- Pipeline: my own reimplementation in `/tmp/ocr_bench/scripts/ocr_pipe.py`, written after reading the repo's `runtime/line_engine.py` and `thai_norm.py`. The repo code was neither run nor imported. Kept from the reference:
  - DB detector preprocessing: white pad, long side 1280, sizes rounded to /32, ImageNet normalisation. Post-processing thresholds from `det.yml` (thresh 0.2, box_thresh 0.4, unclip 1.4).
  - Perspective line crops, then the "flatten" colour prep, then the recognizer at H=48 with input (x-0.5)/0.5.
  - Greedy CTC decoding over [blank] + `rec_dict.txt` (528 entries) + [space].
  - The `config.json` line filters: drop_below 0.8, min_chars 2, junk_below 0.9, junk_invalid 0.05.
  - The Thai canonical form.
  - Left out: orientation/deskew, gap splitting, column XY-cut, fragment removal, the `fix_scripts` W→พ fix-up. Reading order is plain rows (vertical centre, then left to right).
  - Because of these omissions, the numbers can differ from the official runtime. The W→พ fix-up in particular would likely have fixed the `w` for `พ` errors below.
- Runtime: onnxruntime 1.32 CPU EP, 4 intra-op threads, Apple M5 Pro, Python 3.14 venv. Times are the median of 3 runs after one warm-up, covering detection, recognition and post-processing for the whole input.
- Images: 29 unique inputs, 25 from the site plus 4 rendered controls. Pages fetched 1 s apart: home, /admission-schedule/, /academic/scholarships/, /academic-calendar/, /news/, their /th/ versions, three blog posts and /on-stage-events/. All returned 200, none refused.
  - The scholarships and academic-calendar pages carry no content images (text/HTML only).
  - The admission-schedule posters are English only.
  - Most site images are English posters or event photos. Thai text in images is scarce.
- Controls: 4 images rendered with Pillow (raqm shaping on) from known Thai text: Ayuthaya 34 px, Thonburi 30 px, Sukhumvit Set 40 px yellow-on-blue, Silom 20 px. The ground truth is exact.
- Real ground truth: hand-transcribed by me from the images (Read tool, enlarged views), 10 inputs.
  - 3 full images: Bajrakitiyabha, Admission-Bachelor, aaa.
  - 7 crops of single text blocks from busy posters, so reading order does not dominate the score. Crop boxes are given as `@x0,y0,x1,y1` in source pixels.
  - Logo microtext and seal text are excluded from the truth.
  - The full-image truths are written in row-major order (top to bottom, then left to right).
- Metric: CER = Levenshtein(ref, hyp) / len(ref), after NFC and collapsing all whitespace (newlines included) to single spaces. "Overall" pools characters across images (micro). "Line-match" is an order-free secondary score: each truth line is matched against the best substring of the OCR text of the whole uncropped image (Sellers), so it shows what full-image OCR recovers without penalising reading order or extra output.

## Typical errors

- **Thai look-alike consonants**, the most common Thai error. Examples:
  - ศ→ค / ด / ต: "การคึกษา", "นักตึกษา", "ดุริยางคดิลป์"
  - ค→ด: "บุดลากร"
  - ด→ต: "ราชธิตา"
  - ร→ธ: "นิธันดร์"
  - ไ→โ, น→ป, เ→ข on the 6 px headline
- **Thai to Latin script swaps** in display and colour fonts: พ→`w` ("มืออาชีw", "เฉwาะ"), น→`U` and ร→`S` (c3 control "กันยายU", "ค่าสมัคS"; masthead "วารสาSIWลงดUตรี"), ณ→ญ. The reference runtime's `fix_scripts` handles lone W→พ only. I did not include it.
- **Vowels and marks above/below**: few on clean text. On clean controls c1/c2 the score was 0/460 characters, so tone marks are fine there. Errors appear on small or photographed text:
  - ึ→ี ("น้อมสำนีก")
  - ั→ิ, ิ→ึ
  - dropped thanthakhat ์ ("ดุริยางคศิลป", "แคมปิดนตรี" with ์→ิ)
  - ั ่ ู ิ dropped on the photographed screen line
  - one spurious ั inserted ("จากัสวรรค์")
- **Numbers and dates**, the most important failure for knowledge entries:
  - On the admission poster the large coloured day numerals 19, 7, 9 and 10 vanished, leaving "Deadline SEP 2026 NOV 2026 JAN 2027 27 FEB 2027".
  - The round numbers 1-4 were read correctly but removed by the min_chars=2 filter.
  - Punctuation inside numbers gets lost: "1:00 PM"→"100 PM", "(1.5 Hours)"→"(15 Hours)".
  - Letter-spaced dates came out as "2 0 2 6I7 : 0 O P M" in the full MSO poster (fine when cropped: "2026[7:00 PM").
  - Digits rendered as plain text in the controls were all correct.
- **English**: clean body text is good (aaa 4.5%). Bold display caps fail: "ADMISSION"→"ADVISSION", "UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAM"→"UNDERCRADUATE PROCRAM", "AUGUST"→"LEST", "WORLD"→"VVVRLD", "Music"→"Mบsic" (Thai บ for u), "&"→"8"/"S", "XII"→"XIl". Logos and huge outlined letters (MCGP, ITA, MMJ banners, TIJC logo) read as nothing or junk.
- **Full-image layout on busy posters**: whole lines are dropped as low-confidence (MSO date line line-match 0.65, PWB slogan 0.14) or merged across columns, and "SCHEDULE" was missing entirely. Photos of people with incidental text produce no output, which is the correct behaviour. Journal body text at about 6 px (cover_2026jul-view) is mostly dropped, and what is kept is garbled.

## Limits

- The sample is small: 4 controls (815 characters) and 10 real inputs (1,668 characters), of which only 607 characters are Thai-dominant. The site has few images with Thai text, so the real set leans English, and 7 of the 10 real inputs are crops I chose, not whole images.
- I transcribed the ground truth by eye from 600-1000 px web images. Small text, for example the Bajrakitiyabha bottom lines and the 6 px headline, may contain my own mistakes. Logo microtext was excluded by judgement.
- This is a reimplementation without orientation, deskew, gap splitting, column ordering, fragment removal or `fix_scripts`, and it runs on onnxruntime fp32 rather than the default OpenVINO bf16. Results may differ from the official runtime.
- Strict CER on full images includes reading-order effects. The line-match column removes those but ignores junk insertions.
- Controls are synthetic: clean, horizontal, high contrast, with exact raqm shaping. They show an upper bound, not site conditions.
- Timing is a single machine (M5 Pro, 4 threads) and a median of 3 runs.
