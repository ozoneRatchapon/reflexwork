# Abstain experiment 001 - does a calibrated abstain gate beat the shipped thresholds? (2026-11-06)

**Verdict** (added 2026-11-09, plan 010 item 6, from this file's own numbers; no gates were preregistered for this run): a Temper-style calibrated abstain gate does NOT beat the shipped fixed thresholds: at equal recall it answers 59-65% of the negatives vs 50-53% for the shipped rule. Not adopted (`benchmark/NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md`).

Question: the weakest honest point of the product is that a question the manual does NOT cover still gets an unrelated entry about half the time
(`benchmark/field/results/002_*`). katgpt-rs's `temper.rs` (calibration / abstention gate) answers iff `sigmoid(margin / spread) >= tau AND best score >= delta`,
with the spread re-fitted from realised outcomes. Would that help here?

Method (`node benchmark/abstain/experiment.mjs`, read-only, nothing shipped): 424 labelled queries (the 52-query set, the official FAQ in Thai /
English / typo / shortened, the 14 student FAQ items the KB lacks, the 30 staff-only items). "Right-answer positive" = the top entry is a gold entry;
"negative" = nothing should be answered, or the top entry is wrong. Every rule must keep the shipped rule's recall of right answers on the training fold
and then minimise answering on negatives; the parameters are chosen on 4 folds and scored on the 5th (5 folds x 10 repeats, fixed seed).

## Reading
- **Calibrating the existing signals does not help.** The Temper-style rule answers on negatives 59-65 % of the time against 50-53 % for the shipped rule at the
  same recall, and even a logistic regression over all five signals the search exposes (scores, cosines, margins, overlap) only ties it. The information is
  not in the score, so a better threshold cannot recover it. (An earlier hand sweep of the shipped thresholds gave the same answer.)
- **A signal the score does not carry does help a little.** The idf-weighted share of the question's character trigrams that the top entry explains
  ("coverage") lowers answering on negatives from about 50 % to about 43-44 % at the same recall (4 points on all data, 6-7 on the generalisation subsets).
- **Not enough to ship.** The negative sets are small (about 130 queries, so a standard error of roughly 4 points) and 62 KB entries were written from the
  FAQ that supplies the positives. Treat 6-7 points as a lead, not a result. To confirm: more labelled negatives (from the gap log, labelled by staff), then
  add the feature to `kb_search` (JS and Rust twins), change the golden contract on purpose and re-measure.
- **What this says about an NPC-style "brain" for this product:** a modelless calibration loop (katgpt's residual pools re-fitted from outcomes) only pays
  once it has a signal worth calibrating. The next work is signals and labelled outcomes, not a smarter threshold.
